## O1 — Preserve release truth

Close V3.1 from exact primary evidence. Fix the verified staging workflow timeout without changing the owner-approved application candidate. Require two live browser passes, exact release identity, and rollback evidence before calling the release complete.

## O2 — Make positioning specific

A qualified visitor understands within three seconds that Alireza designs, rescues, and stabilizes web systems that must remain operational under real constraints. A generic job-title H1 is not sufficient.

## O3 — Make evidence visible

Quantitative outcomes appear near the top of Home and every value has an evidence ID, source, scope note, and verification date. Unsupported metrics are removed or downgraded.

## O4 — Make ASDEV Audit the primary conversion path

Header, Hero, and Footer use one Audit-first offer with consistent attribution. Evidence is the secondary action. Discover and the portfolio support Audit trust/acquisition rather than competing as equal products.

## O5 — Turn the flagship Case Study into a technical documentary

The reference Case Study presents Before/After architecture, impact, timeline, decisions, trade-offs, and provenance with code-native diagrams and accessible bilingual composition.

## O6 — Keep public surfaces honest and useful

Discover remains useful under slow connections with stable first-viewport media. Blog is not promoted in primary navigation until its explicit publication gate passes; no filler content is created to satisfy layout.

## O7 — Build a distinctive Engineering Editorial system

Reduce unnecessary equal-weight cards, strengthen typography and composition, author mobile layouts deliberately, and use motion only when it explains hierarchy or operational state.

## O8 — Prove quality on the exact candidate

FA/EN, RTL/LTR, 390/768/1440, keyboard, reduced motion, accessibility, SEO, performance, security, and visual evidence pass on one exact SHA. Automated green never substitutes for independent review or human visual judgment.

## Exclusions

- no backend/Admin/Prisma/auth rewrite;
- no standalone Discover product expansion;
- no frozen-project work;
- no fake metric, identity, screenshot, testimonial, client, or evidence;
- no production/content/destructive mutation outside its exact gate.
